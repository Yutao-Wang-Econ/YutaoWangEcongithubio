I am currently a tenure-track assistant professor at the [Institute of Insurance Application and Innovation](https://fiiai.fudan.edu.cn/), [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/en/). 

I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from Singapore Management University. I am an applied microeconomist specializing in labor economics, the economics of insurance, and family economics. My research examines how families make decisions under economic constraints, policy environments, and social norms. I combine structural models with reduced-form methods to study fertility, parental investment, female labor supply, migration, and child outcomes. My current projects investigate the role of social norms in low fertility and low female labor supply, the consequences of internal migration restrictions and family separation, and how insurance and informal family arrangements shape household decisions.
