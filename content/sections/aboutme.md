I am currently a tenure-track assistant professor at the [Institute of Insurance Application and Innovation](https://fiiai.fudan.edu.cn/), [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/en/). 

I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from Singapore Management University. I am an applied microeconomist specializing in labor economics, the economics of insurance, and family economics.

My research studies how families make decisions under economic constraints, institutional frictions, and social norms. I combine structural models with reduced-form empirical methods to examine fertility, parental investment, female labor supply, migration, and child outcomes. My current projects study the institutional roots of low fertility and low female labor supply, the consequences of internal migration restrictions and family separation, and how insurance and informal family arrangements shape household decisions.
